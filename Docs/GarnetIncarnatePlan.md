# Garnet Incarnate — proposed third Earth stage

**Plan only. Garnet is not implemented or selectable in this update.**

**Earth Adept → Adamant Ascendant → Garnet Incarnate.** This uses the earlier Garnet name: the Earth lineage becomes a sovereign of compressed crystal, seismic pressure and immense sword-driven impacts. Keep Earth recognizable through ground stabs, rock fracture, Resolve and protection of allies. Motion should be quick and decisive, with brief heavy impact stops rather than a permanently slow tank.

## Identity and power

Midnight-black tailored royal suit, a compact split cloak and deep garnet facets, with restrained warm gold/ivory edgework. Crystal edges glow at the moment pressure releases. Avoid making the effects look like Hell Flame: use geometric planes, dense stone chips, expanding fault rings and crystalline fracture, without fire or blood imagery.

Use the established Incarnate/Celestial core tier: **650% Moving, 200% Consciousness, 300% Manipulation, 250% Sight/Hearing, +75 mood, ×3.2 melee stat and ×0.04 incoming damage**, rapid recovery, disease protection, evolved Melee Animation knockout protection, ×15 carrying capacity and ×4 individual caravan-speed contribution. Existing armor, Resolve, guard and target mitigation still affect the final result. Treat these as matching baseline stats, not a promise that every sovereign duel is identical.

Prefer resistance to blunt impacts and this mod's forced pushes over another enormous permanent damage reduction. Stronger unrelated control must still clean up skills safely. Temporary guard bonuses should use one strongest eligible guard, never multiply several allied copies together.

## Unlock and save continuity

Propose **300 new unique hostile non-animal kills and fifteen days as Adamant**, followed by a free eight-second **Transcend: Garnet Incarnate** ceremony. God Mode provides a cached-safe **DEV: Transcend-Ready** command. It grants readiness only; the normal ceremony performs the upgrade.

Existing Adamants begin a separate third-stage tracker when this feature ships, including world/caravan pawns. Upgrade the same heart in place. Preserve Earth recovery, Resolve, saved Auto/Off/Non-lethal settings, original clothing, bound equipment quality and relevant remaining cooldowns. Do not stack Adamant and Garnet conditions. Interrupted ceremonies preserve readiness and grant no partial stats or gear.

## Seven proposed combat forms

Ranges/cooldowns below are starting values for live testing. All forms cost no resources and share the current automation/non-lethal controls.

| Form | Proposed action | Starting limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Garnet Severance** | A fast sword draw releases a broad garnet-edged fault crescent. The lane fractures visually behind the cut. Build Resolve once for a successful cast. | 22-cell clear lane; up to 24 enemies; one hit each; 4 s recharge. |
| **Monarch's Advance** | Dash through up to four distinct enemies along checked straight cuts, leaving brief crystalline afterimages and a pressure ridge. Against one elite, perform one stronger arrival cut rather than repeating four hits. | 18-cell approach, eight-cell links; one hit per target; 6 s. |
| **Crownquake** | Stab the ground, twist the sword and release a broad circular seismic crown. One impact staggers and pushes survivors outwards while the pawn recovers into the next form. | Eight-cell radius, 24 targets, up to four safe push cells; 12 s. |
| **Worldspine Crown** | Mark a fixed location, then raise a visual crown of massive garnet/blackstone spires. A short warning precedes one upward impact; Resolve strengthens that same packet. The spires immediately fragment and fade. | 24-cell mark, six-cell burst, 24 targets; 20 s. |
| **Royal Bulwark** | Evolve Adamant's pressure response. Two nearby hostiles trigger a short brace and orbiting crystal plates, protecting the caster and nearby allies while ordinary melee continues. | Three-second guard; four-cell ally radius; strongest guard only; 30 s. |
| **Faultbreak Reversal** | Parry ordinary adjacent melee, shatter into a short stone mirage, retreat to a safe cell and rush back with one decisive counter. Use the lineage's shared defense recovery and counter-depth guard. | Two-second guard window; one counter; 10 s. |
| **Worldheart Collapse** | The grand form: a ground stab marks a huge fractured crown, garnet facets rise around it, then the formation compresses and collapses in one enormous seismic/crystalline impact. Give enemies a clear warning and escape window. | 28-cell mark, one-second warning, ten-cell impact, 32 targets, one hit each; 40 s. |

Wall/closed-door checks protect targets. Knockback uses reachable, unoccupied cells and respects control immunity/recovery. Crownquake does not repeatedly hit a pawn while its ring expands. Spires and crystals are visuals: no spawned walls, terrain changes, roof removal or collision/building damage. The signature collapse must remain readable beneath its grand effect.

## Resolve and automatic combos

Retain the existing three-stack Resolve meter and saved ownership. Successful Severance casts and eligible defended blows build it through the existing gain throttle. Worldspine Crown and Worldheart Collapse can consume Resolve for a bounded bonus on their one committed impact. No separate shatter damage packet or second permanent defensive condition.

Suggested automation: finish the accepted action; use Bulwark under pressure at a safe boundary; Crownquake to open space; Advance to a useful enemy; Severance while building Resolve; Worldspine for a cluster or full Resolve; Worldheart for a high-value cluster or a durable sovereign. Reversal reacts at a valid melee boundary. Allow ordinary melee between forms and preserve the player's attack intent. Never interrupt a player's unrelated movement/work order or stack duplicate suspended attack jobs.

Non-lethal replaces supported injury with the shared bounded suppression/control rules. Do not damage scenery or add uncontrolled secondary damage packets. Individual crystalline shards, afterimages and animation frames cannot deal their own hits.

Retain cached materials, fixed effect/afterimage pools, camera/fog culling and bounded target/witness searches. Rock dust and floor fissures are short-lived visuals. Do not add unbounded projectile/shard spawns or persistent structures.
