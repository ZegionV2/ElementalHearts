# Eclipse Ascendant

Eclipse is the playable six-technique evolution of Lunar. Evolve after 150 unique hostile non-animal kills and ten days as Lunar, or immediately when Celestial Bestow has already fulfilled the milestones. Existing saved Bestow readiness works. The free, interruptible 4.5-second coronation has its own procedural eclipse choreography. God Mode offers `DEV: Evolution-Ready`; it fulfills requirements and leaves the normal Evolve button to perform the ceremony. The cached developer command disappears as soon as God Mode is off.

Evolution upgrades the existing saved condition in place. Personal clothes, transformation state, modes, Moonbrands and active cooldowns carry over. The old Lunar condition is replaced. Existing pre-evolution saves and older Lunar action drivers remain supported.

## Six original techniques

| Technique | Action | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Dusk Crescent | Twelve-cell crimson-edged black sword wave; one impact per hostile. | 4s |
| Umbral Pursuit | Flash through a checked route to the rear or a clear flank, then backstab. The total route is at most twelve cells. | 5s |
| Crimson Waltz | Rotating black crescent in a 4.5-cell radius; one impact per enemy. | 8s |
| Riven Horizon | A 0.35-second warning on fixed ground, then crossing waves rupture a 3.8-cell circle. The two cuts share a single impact per enemy. | 14s |
| Nightfall Reversal | Automatically answers two enemies within four cells: leap away, then rush through a safe opening. | 18s |
| Sovereign Eclipse | A 0.45-second charge followed by a heavy fourteen-cell corridor wave, limited to sixteen victims. | 30s |

Successful opening attacks build up to three caster-owned Eclipse Brands for eight seconds. Riven Horizon and Sovereign Eclipse consume that caster's brands for +15% damage per stack. All six commands are usable manually. Auto Combat flows through the four main forms, uses the reversal under pressure and the finisher on branded targets. On / Off / Non-lethal uses the shared controls. Non-lethal attacks apply suppression instead of wounds. Eclipse's Bestow grants normal Lunar ascension directly through the shared paired ceremony. Both pawns stop and face each other until it finishes.

## Power and eclipses

Normal Eclipse power is below Celestial: 500% movement, 265% melee damage, 65% melee cooldown, 10% incoming damage and +50 mood. It inherits evolved recovery, illness immunity, carrying/travel improvements and immunity to Melee Animation's specific knockout condition.

An actual native eclipse grants **Eclipse Dominion**. Core movement becomes 650%, melee damage 320%, melee cooldown 55%, incoming damage 4% and total ascension mood +75, matching Celestial's core tier. Capacities, recovery, carrying and thermal/solar resilience also rise. Eclipse techniques gain 15% additional power. Celestial's special elemental ward treats an empowered Eclipse as a sovereign peer; empowered Eclipse receives the equivalent ward against lower elemental lineages. This allows a contest between their different kits rather than guaranteeing a winner.

While actively fighting with two visible hostiles within five cells, a transformed, conscious Eclipse has a 15% chance per ten-second check to summon an eclipse. A recent hostile hit or an active combat target establishes combat. The summoned condition lasts 15,000 ticks (six RimWorld hours); a saved 60,000-tick cooldown is shared by the map and summoner. It does not stack, extend an existing eclipse or reroll repeatedly on each hit. Natural eclipses grant the same boost regardless of that summon cooldown. The ordinary native eclipse darkens the local sky and affects solar power. Dominion ends when the actual condition ends or the pawn leaves the map.

## Gear and presentation

Ivory crescent plates, a flat cuirass and a black/crimson embroidered cloak fit all seven body types in south/east/north, with native west mirroring. The indestructible Eventide sword has a native Melee Animation grip/blade profile. A compact bone-white half-mask has a hollow eye and crimson markings. Its strap is behind the head: **the front texture contains no strap**, and the mask does not hide hair or beard. The entire outfit covers the native body and the nudity-related clothing groups.

The subtle six-frame eclipse halo is visible only while transformed, drafted and awake. All combat sequences have eight frames; marks use a small six-frame loop. Movement and leap height are driven by saved C# phases. Cached materials, 96 effect slots, 32 afterimage slots, 128 adornment trackers, camera/fog culling and bounded local searches keep presentation bounded. Attack visuals never cause damage themselves. The eclipse coronation uses procedural cached geometry rather than another element's attack sprites.

Downing or death reverts the pawn to personal clothing and hides regalia, mask and sword in the existing saved holders. Retransformation reuses them. Reverting leaves no summoned gear in inventory. Interrupted movement, warnings, lessons and transformations release their owned actions. Saved impact flags prevent repeated hits after loading mid-action.
