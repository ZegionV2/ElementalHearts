# Ascendant carrying, travel and resilience update

- Normal Ascendants gain a **5× carrying bonus**; Azure Flame, Thunderstorm, Glacial and Cyclone gain **10×**. The native hauling stat and caravan cargo capacity both receive their respective multipliers. Hauling still respects the item's normal stack limit. For a body-size-1 human, caravan capacity becomes 175 kg / 350 kg before other mods' modifiers.
- Walking normal Ascendants contribute **2× caravan speed**, evolved Ascendants **3×**, and ordinary humanlike members **1×**. Mixed caravans average those contributions. Animals keep their vanilla riding/load effects; shuttles keep their original timing. Normal terrain, load, rest and route rules still apply. Caravan previews and actual world travel share the same calculation, with an explanation of the bonus.
- All Ascendants resist diseases, infections, parasites, food poisoning, cryptosleep sickness and toxic buildup. Existing qualifying illness clears on ascension and when loading a save. Immunizable and storyteller disease conditions from other mods are recognized too. Injuries, missing parts, anesthesia, ability conditions and temperature exposure retain their existing behavior. Disciples retain ordinary health rules.
- Evolved Ascendants reject Melee Animation's `AM_KnockedOut` condition. Its installed non-lethal Down outcome completes without forcing a knockout. Existing instances clear on evolution/load. Ordinary Ascendants and other pawns retain Melee Animation knockouts. This adds no Melee Animation dependency and does not change damage, lethal executions, normal downing or death.
- Newly granted disciples and Ascendants start with Auto Combat enabled, including teaching, serum use, Bestow, promotion and awakening. Promotion enables it after copying Water defense state. Saved manual Off/Non-lethal choices survive loading and evolution; the global automation switch remains available. The obsolete per-new-pawn default checkbox is removed, and its old save key remains readable.
- DEV: Evolution-Ready follows God Mode (the skull toggle); DEV: Ascend-Ready also follows God Mode and fulfills disciple milestones without granting ascension. The normal Ascend button starts the ceremony. Each is omitted from fresh command lists when its own mode is off. Their visibility, drawing and input are checked again for cached commands, so an open skills panel cannot keep displaying the developer button.

Existing hediff IDs, ability cooldowns, Water defense/combo keys, transformed gear, saved mastery and artwork are retained. No new gameplay save fields are needed.

Restart RimWorld after updating so it loads the rebuilt assembly.
