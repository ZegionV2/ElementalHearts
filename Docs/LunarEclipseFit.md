# Lunar and Eclipse presentation update

Lunar's head and helmet now sit slightly higher above its collar. The native shared head origin moves the face, helmet, hair and beard together; the adjustment follows the existing apparel manifestation smoothly and returns to the ordinary position on reversion. The lift scales to the pawn's life-stage body width, with appropriate offsets for Male, Female, Thin, Fat, Hulk, Child and Baby bodies.

Moonwake and Eventide appear 30% larger while held. The ordinary game renderer enlarges them around their existing grip point, without changing inventory or ground-icon sizes. Updated Melee Animation profiles enlarge both blades by the same proportion and preserve the grip position, blade start and trail endpoint. Damage, reach and cooldowns are unchanged.

Eclipse's halo is now a thin black/silver and crimson open ring with a genuinely transparent center. Six aligned frames retain its subtle pulsing and travelling silver glints. Its transformed/drafted visibility conditions and the effect pools remain the same.

Both outfits have body-specific fitting in south/east/north with native west mirroring. Fresh native measurements include actual child and baby life stages. Their apparel and headgear definitions allow those developmental stages. Hulk's cloak uses a slightly taller apparel mesh and a matching fitted texture to leave room for the hem. Thin shoulders retain a regal silhouette without a molded chest. Coverage is checked after each texture's render transform, including the side views. Lunar and Eclipse afterimages use the same native mesh dimensions as the outfits rather than multiplying their shapes by the body type a second time.

This update changes presentation and apparel fitting. Existing lineage, transformation, evolution, eclipse, cooldown and ability save data keep their existing keys.
