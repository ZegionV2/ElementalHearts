# Group travel

Celestial Light Travel and Thunder, Hell, Tempest and Aurora Travel gather a party around the actual carrier. The Celestial or Incarnate stays visible in their current form throughout preparation.

1. Select a conscious player-controlled Celestial or Incarnate and activate Travel. First choose a clear, reachable, unroofed launch cell; the carrier may start indoors. For Incarnates, right-click still cycles **Step: Auto → Step: Manual → Travel**. Map Step retains its existing behavior.
2. Use the familiar people/items loading window to select companions, animals and cargo. The carrier is included automatically. Loose allowed cargo uses native hauling rules, including reachability and reservations.
3. The carrier walks to the chosen launch site. Selected companions gather visibly beside that site and are excluded from the available-hauler check, including native loading-lord jobs. Other free colonists haul supplies. When no suitable free hauler is available, the carrier fetches the cargo themselves, delivers it and returns to wait at the launch site. Busy or drafted colonists keep their orders. Downed passengers can be carried to the gathering point. Select the carrier to see gathering and cargo status. Reactivate Travel to edit the manifest.
4. Once everyone and everything is ready, select the carrier and press **Launch living flight**, which uses the shared ivory/gold ascending-pawn icon for every lineage. Choose a world destination, then an arrival action.
5. The party charges, rises and descends through matching elemental effects, supported by a shared sixteen-frame launch vortex and a smoother synchronized lift. No physical pod, pod boarding, pod launch or pod drop animation appears. The world marker is a flying pawn, tinted for the carrier's lineage, including older journeys already in flight. It uses a larger native expanded icon (2.2 scale, at least a 66-pixel square before native expansion) and higher icon priority so journeys remain visible when zoomed out. The world stays visible behind destination menus.

The payload limit is the carrier's current enhanced **caravan carrying capacity**, including passenger body mass, personal gear, inventories and loaded cargo. It follows capacity changes and does not apply the sovereign carrying multiplier twice. Travel costs no fuel or silver and has no cooldown.

## Arrival choices

- **Open land:** form a caravan, where the party retains its supplies and can continue on foot or use **Living flight** to fly onward.
- **Friendly settlements:** native visit/trade and other permitted transport actions.
- **Hostile settlements:** native edge and center attack modes, plus **Attack: choose landing cell on arrival**. Native relationship consequences still apply.
- **Existing maps and sites:** native transporter arrival choices, including specific-cell targeting where the game supplies it.
- **ISEKAI Tower:** its own native floor-entry choices replace generic terrain landing. Floor 1 and legitimately discovered floors remain available; its native floor picker and God Mode rules are preserved. The chosen native floor action is saved with the journey. No generic map is created on the Tower tile.

Chosen-cell enemy arrivals generate the destination normally, then retain the party in a caravan while the player chooses an outdoor cell. Each passenger receives a separate safe cell nearby. Canceling keeps everyone and their cargo in that caravan; select **Choose landing cell** on the caravan to resume, including after saving/loading. If there is insufficient safe space, the entire party remains safely in the caravan.

If Tower entry becomes unavailable during flight—for example, another party occupies its live floor—the arriving party stays safely in a caravan at the Tower. Use the Tower's ordinary caravan menu when entry is available again.

## Cancellation and saves

**Cancel travel preparation** releases the party and safely unloads selected supplies. A new order for the carrier also cancels preparation without consuming that order. An interrupted departure leaves everyone and all supplies at the origin. A carrier who loses their powers, becomes incapacitated or changes faction cannot launch. Roofs and excess payload also block launch.

Original pawn, gear and cargo instances are transferred rather than regenerated. The chosen site, gathering, carrier self-hauling, departure, world flight, pending landing choices and arrival progress are saved. Existing loading pods from the earlier version migrate back to a visible carrier and gathered companions. Existing solo-flight jobs and save keys remain supported so older journeys already in progress can finish.

The earlier [Anomaly-inspired travel proposal](AnomalyTravelEffectsPlan.md) remains shelved. That visual inspiration is reserved for a future new element.
